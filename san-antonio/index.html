Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in San Antonio

A little routine care makes all the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

Small problems rarely stay small for long. Consider a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, inexpensive repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a miserable night.

Typical Heating & Cooling Issues in San Antonio

HVAC systems in this area face a familiar set of problems. Hard-running summers push compressors and capacitors to their limits, and humidity and airborne grit slowly foul outdoor condenser coils, cutting efficiency years before the equipment should show its age.
