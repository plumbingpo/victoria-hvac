AC Repair in Victoria

Most air conditioner breakdowns trace back to one failed part: a weak capacitor, a burned contactor, a seized fan motor, or a slow refrigerant leak. We diagnose it with gauges and meters, not guesswork. Every repair comes with an upfront quote and an honest opinion on repair versus replacement.

  • Warm air coming from the vents
  • Outdoor unit humming but not starting
  • Ice on the refrigerant lines or indoor coil
  • Condensate backing up around the air handler

Furnace Repair in Victoria

Most furnace breakdowns come from a handful of parts: flame sensors, igniters, inducer motors, and control boards. We find the one that failed and fix it, usually in a single visit. We also check for the problems that matter for safety, like a cracked heat exchanger, and give you an upfront price before any work begins.

  • Furnace clicks but never lights
  • Burners light, then shut off within seconds
  • Cold air from the vents in heating mode
  • Loud banging, squealing, or grinding at startup
